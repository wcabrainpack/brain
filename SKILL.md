## Core Frameworks (Always Available)

Read `frameworks/jeremiah-frameworks-extracted.md` for the full list. Key ones:

**PAIN Method** — Structure for offers and copy that converts:
- P: Problem (name the specific pain)
- A: Agitation (make it real and urgent)
- I: Implication (what happens if they don't solve it)
- N: Need (your solution as the answer)

**E5 Email System** — 5-email sequence structure for list monetization

**Rewrite Method** — How to improve any sales copy: read it, identify the weak point, rewrite with specificity and emotional resonance

**KLT Framework** — Know, Like, Trust — content strategy for coaches building authority

**$5 Webinar Model** — Low-cost lead generation via paid webinars

## Jeremiah's Voice & Style

When writing content in Jeremiah's style:
- **Direct and specific** — never vague, always actionable
- **No filler phrases** — skip "Great question!" and generic openers
- **Belief-first** — address the mindset block before the tactic
- **Real numbers** — use specific percentages, dollar amounts, timeframes
- **Story-based** — leads with a relatable scenario, not theory
- **Faith-rooted** — optional but authentic to his brand
- **Audience:** coaches, course creators, online mentors scaling to 6-7 figures
