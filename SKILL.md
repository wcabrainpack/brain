## Core Frameworks (Quick Reference)

### PAIN Method (Copy Structure)
- **P**roblem — Name the specific pain your audience has
- **A**gitate — Make it feel real, visceral, urgent
- **I**nterrupt — Pattern break: "But what if it didn't have to be this way?"
- **N**urture — Bridge to your solution

### E5 Framework (Content-to-Sale Journey)
1. **Expose** — New audience sees short content (reels, YouTube, ads)
2. **Engage** — They opt into email list / register for webinar
3. **Educate** — Value delivery builds trust (emails, content)
4. **Empower** — They feel capable of change with your help
5. **Enroll** — Sales presentation → they become a client

### 3-Part Webinar Formula
- **Part 1 (first 30%):** Vision + mindset + possibilities + personal story → *weed out non-buyers naturally*
- **Part 2 (middle 40%):** Promised content delivery — practical, no holding back
- **Part 3 (final 30%):** Sales pitch → present offer, stack value, CTA

### $5 Webinar / Paid Event Model
- Free registration → $5–$7 VIP upgrade at confirmation page
- VIP = lifetime recording access + 3 bonus trainings
- 3–5 day live event format
- 20–30% of paid attendees convert to main program (vs <5% free webinar)
- **Goal:** Grow email list AND qualify buyers simultaneously

### KLT Framework (Content Strategy)
- **Know:** Short-form content (15–60 sec reels/TikToks) — get found
- **Like:** Podcast, YouTube (10–25 min) — build personality connection
- **Trust:** Webinar/email sequence — deepen belief, move to sale

### Rewrite Method (Copy Critique)
Used in Live Copywriting Rewrite Days:
1. Read existing copy aloud — feel where energy dies
2. Identify: Is it speaking to a real specific pain? Is the outcome vivid?
3. Rewrite headline first — specificity + outcome promise
4. Rebuild bullets around emotional benefits, not features
5. Close with urgency that's honest, not manufactured

### Copy Recipe (5 Elements)
1. **Who** is it for (hyper-specific)
2. **Problem** they have (in their language)
3. **Solution** you offer
4. **Method** (your unique approach)
5. **Dream result** (best-case scenario outcome)

## Voice Calibration

Jeremiah's voice is: **Direct. Warm. Unfiltered. Self-aware. Story-driven.**

He does NOT sound like a polished business coach. He sounds like a friend who's been in the trenches and wants to be real with you.

**Key phrases and patterns:**
- "Here's the thing..." (transition to truth-telling)
- "And I'm gonna shoot straight with you..." (preface to hard truth)
- "I know that I know that I know..." (conviction statement)
- "Get it done. Imperfect action." (the doctrine)
- "All the broke coaches [do X]... the successful ones [do Y]" (pattern contrast)
- Addresses fear directly: "You might be afraid of X. Here's why that's stopping you."
- Uses personal stories: therapy, his mom Sandy, past business failures, video games
- Ends with clear CTA: "If you're a coach who wants [X], here's what to do..."

**Avoid:** Formal language, passive voice, hedge words ("perhaps," "might consider"), corporate speak, empty affirmations without grounding.
