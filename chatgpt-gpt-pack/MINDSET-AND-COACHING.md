## The Foundation: Mindset BEFORE Strategy

Jeremiah is explicit about this in every training: **internal belief creates an income ceiling**. When that ceiling is hit, self-sabotage brings you back down to your "deserved" level.

> "Whatever you're experiencing is tied to your level of belief... You will always get what you say, what you declare, and what you speak out."

The sequence is always: **Mindset → Identity → Strategy → Results**

If results aren't coming, the fix is almost never more tactics. It's almost always belief, self-worth, or fear of visibility.

## The 6 Keys to Selling More Programs

Six prerequisites that separate thriving coaches from struggling ones:

1. **High self-esteem** — You can't sell what you don't believe you deserve to offer
2. **High self-belief** — Confidence in your program is felt through the screen/phone
3. **Commitment to personal development** — Books, coaching, therapy, daily practice
4. **Self-discipline** — Showing up consistently when you don't feel like it
5. **Personal accountability** — Owning your results, not blaming circumstances
6. **Killing perfectionism** — The most destructive force in a coach's business

**On perfectionism:**
> "Perfectionism is insecurity. It's insecurity about what people are going to think about me... it's a losing battle at every single turn."

## Imperfect Action Doctrine ("45% Shipped Beats 100% Unshipped")

**The core principle:** Get it out there. Now. At 45% done. 70% done. Whatever you have.

> "Why am I still on Thinkific even though it's not the best? It does what I need it to do."
> "Speed of execution is the advantage. Done beats perfect every time."

**Practical application:**
- Launch your first webinar before you feel ready
- Post your first Reel before it's "good enough"
- Price your first coaching offer before you have testimonials
- The feedback loop starts AFTER you launch. Not before.

## Do It Scared (Decision Velocity Framework)

The #1 behavior of successful people: making decisions quickly **without having everything figured out**.

> "The number one single greatest behavior for success is the ability to make decisions without having it all figured out, without it feeling perfect... and still making that decision to move forward."

**The practice:**
- Notice fear as a starting signal, not a stop sign
- Make the decision while scared/uncertain
- Build decision velocity as a personality trait
- The people waiting until it's "perfect" will still be waiting in 5 years

## The Mistake = Data Mental Model

From Mark Douglas's *The Disciplined Trader*:

> "Mistakes don't actually exist. They are just data to learn from and grow and to move forward."

**Application:**
- A failed launch = data. What worked? What didn't? Adjust.
- A webinar with 3 attendees = data. Wrong topic or wrong traffic source.
- A bad sales call = data. Which objection tripped you up?
- Get back on the same horse the next day with adjustments.

## The Self-Belief → Sales Conversion Connection

> "If you don't believe in what you're selling, you're not going to sell anything... You cannot get people to sign up for your coaching and courses if you don't believe in yourself."

**Why this is mechanical, not philosophical:**
People *read* disbelief energetically. A coach with lower skill but higher confidence WILL outsell a skilled coach with low confidence.

**The fix:**
1. Coach 1–2 people for free or low cost → get real results
2. Document those results (specific numbers)
3. Raise prices as certainty compounds
4. Loop: Confidence → Price → Results → More Confidence

## Personal Development = Business Development

Your business will never outgrow your internal model of yourself.

**The daily practice Jeremiah recommends:**
- Morning affirmations: "I am making [10x income]. It is easy for me to do so."
- 10x Visualization: "If I was already making [10x], how would I carry myself differently today?"
- Reading: David Bayer, Ryan Holiday, Dr. Joe Dispenza, Marcus Aurelius
- Therapy/coaching for root-level belief work

> "When I wake up in the morning, I think of I've actually gone to 100x my income and I think, if I'm making a billion dollars, what would I do differently? And I start operating in that way."

## Obstacle-to-Blessing Reframe (Stoic Resilience)

Based on Ryan Holiday's *The Obstacle Is the Way* and Marcus Aurelius:

> "I've gotten to the point where it's like, okay, 15 minutes. I can feel it for 15 minutes and I can immediately snap back into where I need to be."

**The practice:**
1. Set a time limit for feeling bad about setbacks (15 min, not 3 days)
2. Ask: "What can I learn from this?"
3. Ask: "What's the opportunity hidden in this obstacle?"
4. Return to work

## The Coaching Business Model

### Coaching vs. Consulting vs. Therapy

| Mode | Key Feature | Who Owns the Result |
|------|-------------|---------------------|
| Coaching | Ask questions → client finds own answers | Client |
| Consulting | Expert advice + helping execute | Consultant |
| Therapy | Licensed; clinical/mental health | Therapist |
| Courses | One-to-many teaching | Student |

**Jeremiah on coaching:**
> "Coaching is all about asking the right questions... the moment you give advice and they follow it badly, you become responsible for the outcome."

### The Group Coaching Model (The Path to Scale)

| Element | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Price | $47–$197/mo (entry), $500–$2k/mo (premium) |
| Format | Weekly live calls (training one week, Q&A the next) |
| Community | Facebook group, School, or Slack |
| Content | Courses as supporting material (not the core product) |
| Scalable | 20–50 clients simultaneously |

**Why group, not 1-on-1:**
- Margins work with paid advertising
- Community creates peer accountability and retention
- Can serve more people for less time
- The $197/mo WCA model: 100 members = $19,700/mo recurring

## The Pricing Ladder

| Tier | Price | Purpose |
|------|-------|---------|
| Qualifier / Front-end | $4.95–$10 | Filters out freebie seekers; covers some ad cost |
| Entry Program | $47–$97/mo | Starter membership |
| Core Program | $147–$197/mo | Main group coaching (WCA model) |
| High-Ticket / VIP | $800–$900/mo | 1:1 strategic access |
| Consulting | $1,500–$15k+ | Done-for-you / deep advisory |

**Key pricing insights:**
- Pricing is a qualification filter — higher price attracts better clients
- Must add high-ticket tier to scale past $30k/mo
- Charge minimum $500–$1,000 for paid ads ROI to work
- "Your first clients are guinea pigs" — charge low to learn, raise prices as certainty grows

**Specialization = 10x Price:**
> "As soon as you specialize something, you can increase the price tag by 10x."
> "Gut health for women 40+" vs. "health coaching" — specificity makes you worth 10x more

## The Navigator Positioning

You don't have to be the world's top expert:

> "You're a navigator. You're a tour guide to somebody who is a little bit further behind you."
> "You just have to know a little bit more than somebody else who knows less than you and is willing to pay to learn what you know."

**The confidence formula:**
- You've solved this problem for yourself
- You've helped 2–3 people solve it
- You have a process
- That's enough to charge and help people

## The Niche-Down Path to Wealth

**Why niching = more income, not less:**
1. Narrow niche → specific problem → specific audience → specific copy → higher conversions
2. Solve same problem repeatedly → expertise compounds fast
3. Expertise → courses → consulting → speaking (passive + premium income)
4. "Gut health for women 40+" is worth 10x more than "health coaching" in the market

**Jeremiah's exact path:**
Generic coach → Niche coach (charge 2–5x more) → Courses from repeated coaching → Group program → Consulting → Platform

## The Graduation Ladder (Turning Coaching Into Courses)

1. Coach 10–20 people on the same problem
2. Notice patterns (same questions, same stuck points)
3. Turn repeated coaching into a course module
4. Send new clients to the course FIRST
5. Coaching becomes strategic; courses handle basics
6. Passive income layer runs alongside coaching

> "I take what I've coached people on the most and turn it into a course so I don't have to repeat myself a bunch of times."

## Discovery Call Protocol

**Format:** 15–30 min. Use Calendly ($8/mo). Minimum 7 days advance scheduling.

**Opening question:**
> "If you could wave a magic wand over one problem in your business right now, what would it be?"

**Session intake questions:**
1. What's your #1 goal for this session?
2. What outcome do you want by the end?
3. What have you tried that's worked?
4. What's the #1 thing in the way right now?
5. What commitment will you make by our next call?

**Disqualification questions (ask early):**
1. 90-day, 6-month, 12-month goals?
2. What's most important to reach those goals?
3. What's prevented you from getting there?
4. Other coaching programs — what worked, what didn't?
5. Are you able to invest capital in growing your business right now?

**Red flags (disqualify immediately):**
- "Every past coach screwed me over"
- Asks for money-back guarantee before you even pitch
- Gets upset with scheduling boundaries
- Lies or evasive in discovery call

> "People that complain every coach in their life screwed them over — they're gonna make you the next villain. Red flags, run away."

## The Question-Led Coaching Model

**A coach talks less than 50% of the time.**

**The question ladder:**
1. "What's happening in your business right now?"
2. "Why has that been hard for you?"
3. "Imagine making 10x your income — what would you do differently?"
4. "What's stopping you from doing that right now?"
5. "What 3 things will you commit to by our next call?"

> "If I tell you what to do all the time, you'll become co-dependent on me for your success."

## The Accountability Architecture

**Three moves per session:**
1. **End with commitments** — "By next call, you'll do A, B, C. Deal?"
2. **Open next session reviewing** — "Did you do it? If not, why not?"
3. **Gate sessions on completion** — "Before scheduling another call, these three things need to be done."

Not punitive. Matter-of-fact. Reference their own stated commitments back to them.

> "I say, 'You're going to have to do this before we meet again, because I'm probably not going to help you beyond you doing that.' That's been an extremely effective way to get people to take action."

## The Ethical Selling Philosophy

- Don't pressure people — find people already saying yes
- Money-back guarantee (real, not fake theater)
- Charge what creates a commitment, not what the market will bear
- Show case studies with real numbers — no vague claims
- "Don't sell crap. Be legit."
- Polarize upfront — repel wrong-fit clients, attract right ones

> "Sales is service. When you flip your perspective — that sales is trust building — it changes the game completely."

## Free Coaching vs. Paid Coaching

> "People don't respect what they don't pay for. It's a fact of life... The entitled people were the free coaching people. The ones that pay you and make a good investment, they want to make good on that."

**The only justified reason for free coaching:** To build your skill before you have confidence to charge.

Once you have 2–3 real results, charge immediately and raise prices as you deliver more results.
