## The Current Model (2024–2026)

**Free-first funnels are mostly dead for cold traffic.** Since ~2024, Jeremiah's recommended front door is a **$4.95–$10 paid micro-offer**.

### The Full Funnel (Current):
```
Daily Reels/Content → build 75% video viewer audience
→ $5/day ads to 1% Lookalike of video viewers
→ $5–$10 Paid Event Registration (class, challenge, or webinar)
→ Email autoresponder (5–7 days)
→ Group Coaching Program Pitch ($197/mo)
→ VIP Upgrade ($900/mo)
→ Consulting ($3.5k–$15k)
```

**Why paid front-end works:**
- Algorithm finds BUYERS, not freebie seekers
- 20–30% of paid micro-event attendees convert to your core program
- Less than 5% of free event attendees convert
- The $5 front-end covers some ad cost while qualifying leads

## The $5 Webinar Model (Most Important Framework)

**The shift:** From free lead magnets → $5–$10 paid entry

**Economics:**
- Cost per $5 registration: ~$6 (slight loss)
- Conversion to $197/mo program: 20–30%
- The math: 100 paid registrations → 20–30 new members → $4,000–$6,000/mo recurring
- Sandy's Menopause Blueprint: 1,000+ sales at $10, 10–20/day consistently

**The offer structure:**
1. $5 registration for a class/event/challenge
2. On registration confirmation: thank you + upsell to VIP (add-on $7–$17)
3. Email sequence: pre-event reminders
4. During event: teach 30/40/30 formula → pitch at the end
5. Post-event: recording access for 72 hours → same pitch
6. After: onboard to group program

**What to charge for on the front end:**
- 1-day live class on a specific topic ("How to Get 5 Coaching Clients This Month — $5")
- 3-day challenge ($5–$10 entry)
- Live workshop/training event ($5–$10)
- Short recorded course + live Q&A ($7–$17)

**What NOT to charge for:**
- Ebooks, PDFs, checklists → still free (list-building tools only, not qualifiers)

## The 30/40/30 Webinar Formula

Every presentation — live or recorded — follows this split:

| Segment | % of Time | Content |
|---------|-----------|---------|
| Opening (Mindset) | 30% | Vision casting, mindset, relatable struggle story, repel bad fits |
| Middle (Training) | 40% | Practical teaching — only ~10-15 min of actual content in 30-min webinar |
| Close (Offer) | 30% | Read the sales page live, direct and confident |

**The Opening:**
- Bold opening hook (don't start with "Welcome everyone!")
- Your credibility story (fast — 60-90 sec max)
- Set the frame: who this IS for, who it's NOT for
- Mention the offer early ("by the end of this, I'm going to invite you to...")
- Mindset section: address the internal blocks before tactics

**The Middle:**
- 3 specific teaching points or strategies
- Each one: context → insight → example → application
- Preview how each connects to the offer
- No holding back — teach the real stuff

**The Close:**
- "Now I want to invite you to take this further..."
- Screen share your sales page and scroll through it live
- Read the page with them (Jeremiah literally does this)
- Tone shift: whimsical/excited in opening → direct/authoritative in close
- Address the top 3 objections explicitly: price, timing, confidence
- Hard close: "Registration closes [DATE]. Here's the link."

**Jeremiah on the close:**
> "In the last part I literally open up the page, screen share it, and scroll down the page and read it with them on camera."
> "If somebody has sat through 66–70% of your webinar already, they're ready and primed for a sales pitch. Yet we get so timid and afraid."

## The VSL Structure (Video Sales Letter)

**Purpose:** Quick, direct offer to warm traffic. 10–20 min max.

1. **Hook** (0–30 sec): Bold result claim. Pattern interrupt.
2. **Problem** (30 sec – 2 min): Agitate the specific pain. "You've tried X, Y, Z. Nothing works."
3. **Why** (2–3 min): Why traditional solutions fail. Root cause.
4. **Solution reveal** (3–5 min): Your method, named. What makes it different.
5. **Proof** (5–8 min): 2–3 specific client results with numbers.
6. **Offer** (8–12 min): What's included. Price. Guarantee.
7. **CTA** (12+ min): "Click below to [action]." Urgency/deadline.

**VSL rules:**
- Straight to the point — no preamble, no "Hey guys welcome to this video"
- Pairs with Deadline Funnel for urgency
- Used when traffic already knows you (warmer audiences)

## The Evergreen Marketing System

**Goal:** Run presentations 24/7 without being live every time.

1. Record a webinar/VSL once
2. Upload to SimuLive or Zoom Automated Webinar
3. Schedule it to broadcast daily at 12pm ET, 3pm ET, and 7pm ET
4. Automated email sequences: registration → pre-event series → post-event → offer deadline
5. Deadline Funnel: 72-hour timer on the sales page starts when they register
6. The timer is REAL — when it expires, they can't access the offer at that price

**Jeremiah:**
> "I haven't done anything live for presentations in forever... It's all pre-recorded, but people are getting access to it on a schedule."

**Keys to making it feel live:**
- Record in a way that doesn't reference today's date explicitly
- Use "This week" or "Coming up" language
- Engage in the chat even on replays (pre-written chat prompts)
- Keep offers time-gated (72 hours after registration)

## The Lookalike Audience Machine (Facebook/Instagram Ads)

**The full sequence for getting $5/day paid traffic that works:**

### Step 1: Build the Video Audience
- Post daily 7–30 second Instagram Reels / TikToks for 2–4 weeks
- Topic: one specific insight per video that speaks directly to your ideal client
- Goal: Get 1,000+ people who watch 75%+ of your videos

### Step 2: Build Custom Audience
- Facebook Ads Manager → Audiences → Create Custom Audience
- Source: Video views
- Type: 75% ThruPlay completion
- Timeframe: Last 30–60 days

### Step 3: Build Lookalike
- From that Custom Audience → Create Lookalike Audience
- 1% Lookalike in US + CA + UK + AU
- This tells Facebook: "Find 19 million people who look like my best viewers"

### Step 4: Run the Ad
- Objective: Conversions (not traffic, not views)
- Audience: That 1% Lookalike
- Budget: $5/day (start small)
- Ad: Speak directly to the 1 problem your paid micro-offer solves
- Destination: $5 paid event registration page

### Step 5: Scale
- When cost per registration ≤ $7 and conversion rate ≥ 20%: increase budget
- Target: $10–$20/day → 100+ registrations/month → 20–30 new program members
- 8x ROAS benchmark: every $1 spent → $8 in program revenue

**Jeremiah:**
> "Facebook can find me 19 million people on Facebook and Instagram that have similar data points to who watched through 75% of my videos."

## The 72-Hour Urgency Window

**Why 72 hours works:**
- Long enough that people can actually find time to attend
- Short enough to create real urgency
- No fake countdown timers — the access literally expires

**Implementation:**
1. Registration confirmation: "Access expires in 72 hours"
2. Email at 48 hours: "24 hours left to watch"
3. Email at 3 hours: "3 hours left"
4. Deadline Funnel on the sales page: timer starts on registration

**Jeremiah:**
> "I like that 72-hour time limit for urgency of action... limit the amount of time they have to watch it and you'll encourage hundreds more people to actually consume your content."

## Topic Selection Formula (What Webinar to Run)

Pick the topic people **will actually register for**, not the topic you think they need.

**High registration topics:**
- "How to get [X] new coaching clients this month"
- "How I made $[X] in 30 days with [specific method]"
- "The 3 things stopping coaches from growing past $5K/mo"
- "How to run Facebook/Instagram ads that actually work for coaches"
- "How to start making money coaching in the next 90 days"

**Low registration topics (avoid for cold traffic):**
- "How to build a funnel"
- "My email marketing strategy"
- "Webinar best practices"

> "It needs to be a beeline for your paid offer... I've hosted webinars I thought were great, and very few people showed up — because the topic wasn't enticing enough."

**Formula:** The title must speak directly to the result your buyer wants MOST and must lead naturally to your paid program.

## Content Strategy (What to Post to Build Audience)

### Daily Content Formula:
- One 7–30 second Reel or TikTok per day
- One idea. One problem solved. No preamble.
- Spend ≤ 5 minutes total.
- Post between 7am–7pm.

**The algorithm prioritizes:**
- Completion rate: shorter = easier to complete = more distribution
- Saves + shares: teach something worth saving
- Comments: ask a question at the end

**Content categories (rotate weekly):**
1. Tactical tip (one specific strategy)
2. Mindset reframe ("Stop thinking X. Start thinking Y.")
3. Client result story (specific numbers)
4. Behind the scenes (show your process)
5. Polarizing take ("Here's why [popular advice] is wrong")

**The Content-to-Presentation Pipeline:**
- Content (free, no email required) → builds trust and audience → drives to presentation
- Presentation (requires registration or payment) → converts to clients
