## 1. The Rewrite Method (Dan Kennedy / Gary Halbert / Sandy Krakowski Trinity)
**Source:** Feb 2 WCA call, webinar transcripts
**Usefulness: 5/5**

**His exact prompt:**
> "Rewrite this sales copy with the ideas of Dan Kennedy and Gary Halbert and Sandy Krakowski in mind. Keep the same context, don't remove any copy, and write a final sales letter."

**What each brings:**
- **Gary Halbert:** Conversational, news-focused hook style
- **Dan Kennedy:** Direct response formatting, fascination bullets, authority positioning
- **Sandy Krakowski:** Connection, empathy, faith-forward warmth

**Copy principles:**
- Charge $500–$1,000 minimum (that's the profit margin number for paid ads)
- Don't devalue with $100–200 pricing
- Social proof via testimonials with specific results ($40K/mo, $140K/mo)
- Use urgency but back it up with real deadlines

## 2. The Landing Page Anatomy (5-Section Formula)
**Source:** `sales-copywriting/2024-12-01_Sales Letter & Landing Page Copywriting Made Easy with A.I - Lesson 1.txt`
**Usefulness: 5/5**

**Structure (in order):**
1. **Headline** — Short promised outcome. Bold. Large. Always at the top.
2. **Story Section** — 2–3 paragraphs. Emotional, relatable narrative before the offer. Real photo included.
3. **What's Included** — Bullet points of outcomes, learnings, access. Optional soft CTA midway.
4. **Testimonials** — Optional (your own story substitutes). For high-ticket, place before "What's Included."
5. **Call to Action** — Final purchase/register link. Optional bio at the bottom.

**Key rules:**
- White background, black text. Simple. Launch fast.
- Always start in a Google Doc before touching any platform.
- "99% of people miss the story section."

**Jeremiah's Quote:**
> "There's no single greatest aspect to marketing your business than the landing page copy... The number one lever you can pull that makes the biggest impact."

## 3. The Copy Headline Formula (Result + Objection)
**Source:** `sales-copywriting/2023-08-10_Sales Copy Mastery With Chat GPT - 3.txt`
**Usefulness: 5/5**

**Formula:**
> **[Promised Result]** even if **[Main Objection]**
> *Example: "You will be able to earn six figures even if you've never sold anything before."*

**Anatomy of a great headline:**
1. Big bold promise (outcome)
2. Address the objection their mind immediately fires
3. Pull them into the story section

**Jeremiah's Quote:**
> "The result result objection. And then a compelling couple of paragraphs of story. That's what separates effective copy."

## 4. The Story Hook (Agitate → Empathize → Solution)
**Source:** `sales-copywriting/2023-08-10_Sales Copy Mastery With Chat GPT - 3.txt`
**Usefulness: 5/5**

**Formula:**
```
"Are you tired of [PAIN/PROBLEM]?
I know what it's like. I've been there.
[2-3 sentences of struggle narrative].
And then I found [SOLUTION]."
```

**Key rules:**
- This section alone decides whether people keep reading
- Fifth-grade reading level
- Short paragraph breaks (skimmable)
- Agitate the pain → empathize → tease the solution

**Jeremiah's Quote:**
> "You want them to be able to see themselves — the struggle, remind them of that struggle, you're agitating that struggle... before they ever get to the 'how,' they'll feel and see themselves experiencing the final result."

## 5. The Swipe File + AI Remix Method
**Source:** `sales-copywriting/2023-08-10_Sales Copy Mastery With Chat GPT - 3.txt`
**Usefulness: 4/5**

**Process:**
1. Save competitor sales letters into Evernote (hundreds over time)
2. Extract best headlines/hooks into a text doc
3. Feed to ChatGPT with your customer context + real survey responses
4. Command: *"Rewrite the following sales letter based on the above context info and survey responses"*
5. Layer in actual customer language (their exact words from comments/DMs)
6. Launch → get feedback → repeat

**Jeremiah's Quote:**
> "I literally have hundreds of sales letters I've saved over the last year. It's a big reason why we're successful — I save all these different sales letters."

## 6. Sell What They Want, Deliver What They Need
**Source:** `sell-more-programs/2022-02-24_Day 3_ Sell More Paid Programs In 2022.txt`
**Usefulness: 5/5**

**The distinction:**
- **Marketing:** Lead with what they WANT (outcome, dream, transformation)
- **Delivery:** Give them what they NEED (mindset, process, hard truths)

**This is NOT bait-and-switch when done ethically.** You're promising the destination and delivering the map. The destination is real. The road is harder than expected.

**Jeremiah's Quote:**
> "If I had told you this was a course focusing on mindset, messaging, and offer building, you would NOT have signed up... You have to lead with what they want so you can lead them to what they need."

## 8. The PAIN Method
**Source:** WCA calls, VIP sessions, webinar transcripts

**The 4-stage emotional arc in copy/presentations:**
- **P** — Problem (what they're suffering from)
- **A** — Amplify (make the pain feel real and urgent)
- **I** — Implication (what happens if they DON'T solve it)
- **N** — New solution (your offer as the answer)

**Jeremiah's Core Quote:**
> "You have to understand what is the pain and pleasure that the prospect is looking to avoid and experience."

---

## 9. The E5 Framework
**Source:** VIP calls, WCA training

**The 5 Exposures:**
1. **Exposure 1** — First encounter (ad, social post, friend referral)
2. **Exposure 2** — Content consumption (YouTube, podcast, blog)
3. **Exposure 3** — Opt-in / Lead magnet (email list)
4. **Exposure 4** — Webinar / Presentation
5. **Exposure 5** — Purchase decision

**Key insight:** People need an average of 5–7 touchpoints before buying. Design your marketing system so all 5 exposures happen naturally within a few weeks.

## 10. The "Who First" Marketing Method
**Source:** `sell-more-programs/2022-02-24_Day 2_ Sell More Paid Programs In 2022.txt`
**Usefulness: 5/5**

**The counter-intuitive insight:** Start with WHO, not WHAT.

**Process:**
1. Define psychographics (not demographics): What brands, music, TV shows? How do they communicate?
2. Survey directly: "What's the #1 thing stopping you from [desired outcome]?"
3. Mirror their exact words back in copy, ads, and offers
4. Use AI to write content aimed at that specific WHO
5. Let the WHO drive all social media content, landing pages, ads

**Jeremiah's Quote:**
> "When you start with who first, your sales become automatic and nearly effortless. When focused on what you're selling, it's a nearly impossible uphill battle."

## 20. The Six Steps to Six Figures Roadmap
**Source:** Synthesis across WCA, coaching, sell-more-programs corpus
**Usefulness: 5/5**

1. Identify WHO you serve (narrow niche, specific problem)
2. Write copy that speaks to what they WANT (not need)
3. Create a low-ticket entry offer ($4.95–$10 webinar/course)
4. Run $5/day ads to build lookalike audience
5. Host live presentations (3–5 day challenge or webinar)
6. Upsell to group coaching program ($47–$197/mo) recurring

**The math:** 100 buyers at $5 → 20–30 join $197/mo → $4,000–$6,000 monthly recurring from one campaign.

## 23. The 3-Part Webinar Formula
**Source:** WCA training, webinar transcripts

**The three phases:**
1. **Opening (Hook)** — Bold claim + story of your transformation
2. **Training (Value)** — Teach 3 specific strategies/insights
3. **Close (Offer)** — Natural invitation to the paid program

**Anti-webinar insight:** Jeremiah NEVER separates content from pitch. Program mentions are woven organically throughout. The pitch is a persistent thread, not a section.

## 24. The $5 Webinar Model
**Source:** VIP calls (Nov 2025), WCA training (Jan 2026)

**The shift:** Away from free lead magnets → charge $2–$10 on the front end.
- Preferred price: $5 for most coaches
- Why: The algorithm finds BUYERS, not freebie seekers
- Economics: $6 cost per $5 registration = $1 loss, BUT buyers convert at 5–10x free leads
- Sandy's Menopause Blueprint: 1,000+ sales at $10, 10–20/day

**Jeremiah's Quote:**
> "We're moving away from free offers on the front end, and we're going towards these $5 or $3 or $10 type offers."

## 28. The Webinar 30/40/30 Split (Vision → Training → Sell)
**Source:** `webinars/2025-01-27_7 Figure Webinars - Lesson 4.txt`

| Segment | % | Content |

## 29. The Topic Selection Formula
**Source:** `webinars/2025-01-14_Webinar Lesson 2_Part1.txt`
**Usefulness: 4/5**

Pick the topic people *will actually show up for*, not the topic you think they need.

**High show-up:** Getting clients, starting a business, running ads, Instagram growth
**Low show-up:** Funnels, webinars, email marketing (too meta — they don't know they need it yet)

The topic must be a "straight shot" to your paid offer.

**Jeremiah's Quote:**
> "It needs to be a beeline for your paid offer... I've hosted webinars I thought were great, and very few people showed up — because the topic wasn't enticing enough."

## 40. Do It Scared (Decision Velocity Framework)
**Source:** `youtube-transcripts/2025-09-09-doing-it-scared-the-secret-to-peak-performance.md`
**Usefulness: 4/5**

The #1 behavior of successful people: making decisions quickly without having everything figured out.

1. Stop waiting for perfect conditions
2. Make the decision while scared/uncertain
3. Treat discomfort as a starting signal, not a stop sign

## 53. The Copy Recipe

Jeremiah's process for producing any piece of copy:

## 54. The Question-Led Coaching Model
**Source:** `coaching/2022-03-02_Coaching - Lesson 2.txt`
**Usefulness: 4/5**

A coach talks less than 50% of the time. The goal is to lead clients to their own answers through strategic questions.

**The question ladder:**
1. "What's happening in your business right now?"
2. "Why has that been hard for you?"
3. "Imagine making 10x your income — what would you do differently?"
4. "What's stopping you from doing that right now?"
5. "What 3 things will you commit to by our next call?"

**Jeremiah's Quote:**
> "Coaching is where you ask questions and the answers reveal themselves... If I tell you what to do all the time, you'll become co-dependent on me for your success."

## 55. The Red Flag Client Screening System
**Source:** `coaching/2022-03-02_Coaching - Lesson 6.txt`
**Usefulness: 4/5**

**Discovery Call Questions:**
1. 90-day, 6-month, 12-month goals?
2. Most important areas needed to reach those goals?
3. #1 thing preventing you from getting there?
4. What's worked, what hasn't?
5. Other coaching programs — what worked, what didn't?
6. Are you able to invest capital in growing your business right now?
7. (Per-session) If you could wave a magic wand over one problem, what would it be?

**Red Flags (disqualify immediately):**
- Gets upset with scheduling boundaries
- "Every past coach screwed me over"
- Asks for money-back guarantee on coaching before starting
- Lies or is evasive in discovery call

**Jeremiah's Quote:**
> "People that complain every coach in their life screwed them over — they're gonna make you the next villain. Red flags, run away."

## 56. The Accountability Architecture
**Source:** `coaching/2022-03-02_Coaching - Lesson 2.txt`
**Usefulness: 4/5**

**Three accountability moves:**
1. **End every session with commitments** — "By next call, you'll do A, B, C. Deal?"
2. **Open next session reviewing those commitments** — "Did you do it? If not, why not?"
3. **Gate more sessions on completion** — "Before scheduling another call, these three things need to be done."

Not punitive. Matter-of-fact. Reference their own stated commitments.

**Jeremiah's Quote:**
> "I say, 'You're going to have to do this before we meet again, because I'm probably not going to help you beyond you doing that.' That's been an extremely effective way to get people to take action."
