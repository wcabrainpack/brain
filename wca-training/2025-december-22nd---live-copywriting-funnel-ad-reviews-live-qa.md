Facebook/Instagram Ads: Targeting, Setup, and Strategy

-
Campaign objective selection

Prefer Conversions (Website Conversions)

- Event optimization: Add to Cart often better than Purchase for initial learning

-
Targeting shifts and limitations

Removal of religious and personality (e.g., Tony Robbins) targeting options

- Broad targeting with specific copy to let algorithm find the right audience

- Instagram often better for targeting reach

-
AI assistance for ads

meta.ai for brainstorming targeting ideas

- Caveat: AI suggestions may reference deprecated options (e.g., faith-based targeting)

-
Detailed targeting ideas (non-religious proxies)

Self-help, stress management, mindfulness, personal development

- Health/fitness/wellness, motivational speaking

- Acupressure/acupuncture as adjacent interests (EFT relation)

-
Lookalike audiences and seed lists

Upload customer lists (CSV) to create custom audiences and lookalikes

- Ideal LL size: 10–20M, let algorithm optimize within

-
Practical tips

Avoid managing ads on phone; use desktop Ads Manager

- Remove redundant detailed targeting when using robust lookalikes

-
Creative/copy alignment

Ensure ad and landing page clearly speak to intended audience (e.g., Christians) so algorithm self-selects

Lead Gen and Offer Strategy

-
Free vs. paid opt-ins

Shift from “free” to $5 micro-offers for filtering and commitment

-
Entry-point offers

Use a low-ticket ($5–$10) class/offer to build trust, then upsell membership

- Avoid sending cold traffic directly to memberships (low conversion)

-
Offer repackaging

Convert prior $300–$390 class frameworks into a $10 intro experience (trim scope, keep wow factor)

- Include on-demand lessons and clear CTAs to join membership

- Email autoresponders to nurture and convert after purchase

-
Scope and overwhelm

Deliver enough to excite and create desire for more; avoid overloading the intro product

Compliance, Regions, and List Hygiene

-
Email regulations overview

Canada (CASL): implied consent window commonly cited as two years after last purchase/interaction

- EU (GDPR): broader data/privacy obligations; enforcement variability

- US: unsubscribe requirement focus; less stringent than EU/CAN (state variations like CA)

-
Practical list management

Keep unsubscribe links; remove long-term inactives to protect deliverability/costs

- Be cautious with Europe/Canada if concerned; target primarily US to simplify
