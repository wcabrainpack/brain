**Topics Discussed:**

Membership and offer building

- Simplifying multi-tier membership to single tier

- Course creation: teaching at church, recording separately for group

- Launch plan: 30-day runway, email list reactivation

- Sales page copy iterations and prompt engineering effects

- Marketing hook vs. utilized content distinction

- Front-end “bait” offers vs. core transformation

- Examples: “become a famous worship leader” as hook; conflict-focused marriage branding (“Fighting Fair,” boxing gloves)

- Emphasizing problem-forward, colorful copy over solutions

- Emergency framing and bold hooks (e.g., “Zuckerberg is stealing from you”)

Ads strategy and webinar runway

- Recommended lead time: 2–3 weeks (shorter once algorithm is dialed)

- Registration curves: spike at launch and near event; lull mid-run

- Budget pacing: heavy first week, taper mid, ramp final weeks

- Case note: client “Sandi Krakowski” webinar with ~600 registrations

Boosting posts vs. Ads Manager

- Instagram: boosting can work; special boost type to drive profile visits

- Facebook: avoid boost; lower quality traffic and less goal alignment

- Prefer Ads Manager for precise objectives (event responses, conversions)

- Using lookalike audiences; leveraging page engagement as source

- Daily budget vs. lifetime budget: daily preferred for control

- Pixel and proper conversion setup importance

Event promotion specifics

- Local event ads (pumpkin show)

- Facebook event response objective

- Strategy: take high-performing organic post, reuse the video, rewrite copy with CTA, run as ad to lookalikes

Instagram boosting cost tip

- 30% fee when funding via mobile app (App Store/Play Store)

- Load ad funds via desktop (Instagram/Meta billing) to avoid fee

- Brief context: Fortnite legal precedent enabling off-app payments

Turning organic content into ads

- Identify best-performing posts/reels

- Reuse media in Ads Manager; change copy to include CTA and headline

- Dual path CTA: comments trigger DM automation; Learn More link to landing page

- Example funnel:

                        -  $4.95–$17 paid classes (e.g., menopause optimization)

                        - Conversion to $97/mo coaching community (price planned to increase to $247)

                        - Limited-time pricing extensions and email campaigns

Combined organic + paid ecosystem

- Ads alone can sell; organic alone often insufficient

- Best results from integrating both

- Plan to productize micro-lessons (e.g., 15-minute Instagram ads tactic) into $5 classes

Platform comparisons and engagement behavior

- TikTok ads feasible; Ads Manager similar to Facebook’s

- TikTok often yields higher engagement due to anonymity

- X (Twitter) like-privacy increased engagement

- Instagram/Facebook public likes may suppress engagement behavior

Call-to-action best practices

- Ensure every promoted post/ad has a clear CTA

- Avoid “vanity” boosts without conversion path

Product and funnel design philosophy

- Start simple; add tiers later

- Model successful market examples, then add your unique spin

- “Best stuff forward” in free/lead content to attract and nurture

- Borrowing ideas ethically; originality vs. proven patterns

- Obedience/purpose-driven creation alongside marketing rigor
