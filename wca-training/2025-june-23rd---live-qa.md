**Topics Discussed:**

### Personal Productivity & Mindset

-
Procrastination & avoidance

Getting “caught up” serving clients instead of building own business

- Comfort-zone work vs. growth work

-
Discipline & delayed gratification

Taking weekends off to reset

- Scheduling creative vs. disciplined work blocks

- “Discipline is freedom” concept (Jocko Willink)

-
Self-care & emotional regulation

Treating business growth as self-care

- Nervous-system resets (movement, hot shower, shaking, shouting)

-
Validation & love-language awareness

Seeking external praise from sales/clients

- Re-wiring reward centers to internal validation

-
Therapy & mental-health tools

EMDR, working with therapists

- “Spirit of gluttony” & numbing behaviors (food, study, prayer, etc.)

### Business Strategy & Prioritization

- Balancing client fulfillment vs. business growth

- Setting clear priorities (landing pages, sales copy, ads before fulfillment)

- Creating low-ticket + core + VIP offer stack

- Building evergreen assets gradually (recording lessons weekly)

- Using collaboration/partnership models to deliver & market offers

### Marketing & Sales Tactics

-
Lead-generation assets

E-book + 5–day video sequences

- Challenges & mini-courses

- Urgency windows (24-–72-hour download links)

-
Email follow-up frameworks

Consumption reminders → direct pitch alternation

- Mirroring prospect answers in follow-up videos

-
Webinars & video sales letters

Wearing collared shirts, adjusting backgrounds for authority

-
Funnel rotation

Run one offer 2–3 weeks, then swap headline/angle to lower ad costs

-
Paid-ads testing

Budgeting small test spends, monitoring CPL rise

-
Direct-message automation

Instagram “DM the word X” → ManyChat/GHL flows

- Progressive question sequences + personalized video/audio reply

-
Audience positioning

Speaking to primary female demographic vs. macho energy markets

- Opposing-energy approach (coach drives sale when audience is passive)
