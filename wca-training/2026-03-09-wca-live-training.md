## 🎯 Key Topics Covered

### 1. Mindset: Making Agents as Easy as Brushing Your Teeth
- The real barriers to adoption aren't technical — they're about fear of wasting time, hurting your brand, and not being able to handle growth
- Goal: make using agents as automatic as brushing teeth or recording content
- Claude Opus 4.6 and ChatGPT 5.4 Thinking know more than you need to about building these businesses — you don't need to understand it all

### 2. Goal-Setting Framework: Action Structuring (Not Just Goal Setting)
- Map out: 5 years → 12 months → 6 months → 30 days
- Connect the dots between levels with specific behaviors and actions
- Be **tight** (specific/accountable) on 30–90 days; **loose** on 5-year vision
- Jeremiah's 12-month goal: $10M; 5-year: $50–100M business valuation
- Not reaching 30-day goals = flag; not reaching 5-year goals = okay

### 3. Identifying the Bottleneck
- Where is the constraint? Where do things stall or slow down?
- Jeremiah's example: He spent 30 days building a 75-agent team instead of launching an offer
- Next 30 days: launch another 15–30 agents to structure next 6–12 months

### 4. The "Wound of Being Seen"
- Jeremiah identified that fear of public visibility was keeping him playing small
- Afraid of disappointing people, being taken advantage of, hurting family
- Naming the wound gave him the ability to build out the infrastructure for scale

### 5. Enterprise Agent Opportunity
- $50,000–$100,000/month agent builds for large companies
- Connected with a contact who was at Mar-a-Lago with Trump — exploring enterprise-grade agent infrastructure
- Distinction: WCA members get help via VIP; enterprise clients pay $50–100k

### 6. Alex Hermozi's Agent Model (Steal This Idea)
- Hermozi launched a $1,000/month offer with dedicated AI agents trained on his system
- Agents for: sales, marketing, landing pages, each business area
- Community + bi-weekly/monthly Zoom calls
- Lesson: pivot your mentorship into an **agentic service**
- "The agents do it better, and you come in to direct and guide"

### 7. Lovable as the Gateway Drug to Agents
- Start with Lovable to build small AI-powered tools
- Use Manus for task automation
- Next steps for members: Perplexity Computer, OpenClaw on Hostinger, DM Jeremiah on Instagram

### 8. Content Strategy + Agents
- Jeremiah's OpenClaw video got 8,000 views — best ever — because his agent told him what to say based on algorithm analysis
- Agents can analyze trends, learn your content, and tell you what the algorithm will grab
