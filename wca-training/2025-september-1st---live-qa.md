Podcast update and mindset theme

- Upcoming episode: “The most important habit in your business”

- Handling tech failures, no-shows, failed launches

- Viewing setbacks as part of success; performance mindset beyond mental health

- Training your mind not to quit when things go wrong

Shonda’s case study: events, offers, and numbers

- Masterclass metrics: ~120 signups; 15–20 live attendees

- VIP sales: 17 total (7 pre, ~10 during live)

- Main paid offer (one-day workshop at $197): 0 sales; one financing request declined

- Live-attendance incentive: free PDF gift delivered via Zoom link

- VIP buyers: only 3 attended live; others may have purchased for replays/bonuses/resources

- Upcoming shift: pitch the membership (work-at-your-own-pace) instead of a one-day workshop

- 1:1 path: booking a call with a VIP who can’t sit for a full-day workshop; likely membership fit

Show-up and follow-up for 1:1 calls

- No-shows on complimentary calls: sending “still here” and “missed you—reschedule” emails

- Reassurance: no-shows are about the prospect’s life, not your failure

- Increasing show rates: make the offer specific/urgent and anchored to a clear pain point

- Using intake questions to collect language and desired clarity/outcomes

Diagnosing offer disconnects

- Interpreting numbers: 66% VIP conversion of live attendees is strong

- Likely disconnect between VIP offer and the separate paid workshop offer

- Possible buyer logic: “I’ll see what VIP delivers before committing again”

- Ways to improve: stronger promise/outcome, bonuses, deadlines, guarantees, price point alignment

- Using AI in programs as a value multiplier (“sweeten the deal”)

One-on-one sales skills and ethics

- Course reference: “Mastering Individual Sales: Guide to 1:1 Closing”

- Rapport and discovery: ask about family/work/fun (acronym approach), build connection first

- Mirroring energy and transferring enthusiasm; sales as transference of emotion/belief

- Eliciting hopes, dreams, and desires (family, fun, life, business) and aligning the offer as the vehicle

- Customizing the offer in real time to the person’s goals and language

- Persuasion vs manipulation: intent matters; ethics, boundaries, and protecting yourself

Copywriting and landing page optimization

- Make headlines outcome-based and future-paced (not narrowly tied to just the free class)

- Cast the big vision (top 1% outcome) even in free offers; explain scope during delivery

- Ensure downstream offer (membership/program) carries a stronger promise than the opt-in page

- Color psychology: brighten landing page colors beyond brand palette when useful for conversion

- Avoid narrowing the audience unnecessarily (e.g., “for coaches, speakers, entrepreneurs” if not needed)

- Encourage aligned headlines that bridge free class → paid offer outcome

- Use testimonials and clearer success outcomes on membership page; refresh dated copy and visuals
