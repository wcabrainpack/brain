**Topics Discussed:**

Black Friday strategy

-
Core approach

Promote one core offer (or two: low-ticket + high-ticket)

- Avoid overcomplicating or too many separate “classes”

- Use discounts, temporary bonuses, unique time-limited deals

- Send daily or every-other-day emails during BF/Cyber period

- Pre–Black Friday promos work well

-
Offer examples

Open usual webinar-only membership deal to full email list for BF

- Example: Women’s menopause group discounted from $247/mo to $77/mo

- No BF discount for the stocks community this time

-
Creative/Ads for BF

Strong results with AI video via Sora (AI-generated spokesperson/cameo)

- Combining Sora videos with real-person videos

- Static graphics underperforming unless tightly offer-aligned

- Using ChatGPT to ideate and generate ad concepts and graphics

- Reusing AI-created characters in multiple ads

-
Accessing Sora (geo considerations)

Using VPNs (NordVPN, Surfshark, ExpressVPN) to access ChatGPT/Sora if geo-blocked

- No invite code needed now

-
Key reminder

Don’t overthink Black Friday; always promote something, even if basic

Ads not working: diagnosis and fixes

-
Mindset and frequency

80% of campaigns don’t work initially; avoid panic and troubleshoot

-
First checks

Ad copy clarity, offer presentation, and empathy-based review

- Include price in ads for paid offers to filter “free-only” clickers

-
Case studies

Rick Pino Texas tour (2021): ads tanked until “$20 General Admission” was added; then 5:1 ROAS

- $4.95 Instagram Ads Playbook: initial ads failed due to no price shown; adding price improved performance

-
Why showing price helps

Repels non-buyers (“free-ple”) and trains algorithm toward buyers

-
Landing page optimization

Clarify what buyers get; avoid confusing or off-putting elements

- Switch from limited access (3-day pass) to lifetime access if it blocks conversions

- Maintain urgency on backend offers (e.g., 72-hour deadlines)

-
Funnel instrumentation

Map full journey: ad → LP → checkout → webinar/training → email follow-up → core offer → program engagement

- Identify bottlenecks at each step

-
Pixel/Conversion goals

Use multiple events: InitiateCheckout on scroll, AddToCart on CTA, Purchase via Zapier

- Add “Add to Cart” intermediary page/button to capture higher-intent signal

- Optimize ads to AddToCart for low-ticket offers where Purchase objective underperforms

- Example stats: ~20% reach bottom, ~7% click Add to Cart, ~50% of ATC purchase

-
Offer tweaks

Add/adjust bonuses

- Update testimonials, video, copy dynamically

- Improve email follow-up to convert into coaching program

-
Belief and buying pressure

Price rarely the true issue; focus on increasing belief/trust/urgency and FOMO

- Two levers: sweeten the deal; deepen connection

-
Trust-building

Reference: The Speed of Trust (Stephen M.R. Covey)

- Trust tactics are neutral; integrity determines ethical use

- Overcome perfectionism and fear of offending; present confidently

-
CUB score framework

Reduce: Confusing, Unbelievable, Boring

- Indoctrinate (educate) to move bold promises from “unbelievable” to believable

Micro offer strategy (low-ticket front-end)

-
Rationale and benefits

Replace/free complement to free webinars/challenges

- $5–$10 micro offer filters out free-only audience

- Qualifies higher-quality prospects; better ad spend efficiency

-
Content format

“Mini-course” = often a single 60-minute webinar/training

- Example upcoming: AI to create video content

-
Economics

Don’t expect profit on the $5–$10 front end alone

- Profit comes from backend program offer (presented in training + follow-up)

-
Build components

Landing page + sales copy

- Training video

- Email autoresponder sequence and deadline tool (e.g., Deadline Funnel)

- Payment cart/membership delivery, pixels/conversions, ads, and content promos

-
Creation workflow (starter checklist)

Write landing page copy

- Record training video

- Write/send follow-up emails

- Set up cart, pixels, deadlines, and ads

Live coaching Q&A: VIP Black Friday offer (Shonda)

-
Offer positioning feedback

Initial copy scored high on “boring” in CUB; needed a stronger hook

- Use empathy-driven, direct, energetic copy styles (inspired by Gary Halbert/Dan Kennedy/Sandy Krakowski)

- Example headline improvement: “Read this if your ‘someday’ book still isn’t written”

- Contrast language: eliminate fluff (journaling prompts, vision boards); promise a deadline/roadmap/accountability

-
Tone adaptation via ChatGPT

Blend direct-response tone with playful, empathetic, conversational voice

-
Pricing and payment plans

Suggested price: $797

- Offer 2–3 payment options to increase total sales volume

- Don’t limit payment timing for arbitrary accounting constraints; discuss deferral with accountant if needed

- Contracts recommended for >$3–4K; not necessary at ~$797

- Handle defaults: clear terms; consider pause/remaining balance as a middle-ground

-
Personal development note

Address conflict avoidance; set boundaries without “fighting”
