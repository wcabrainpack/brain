Ads and boosting discussion

-
Facebook boosting myths

Claim: Boosting hurts organic reach — debunked

- Example: Mom’s page has strong organic and boosts/ads; no harm

- Caveat: YouTube may throttle organic when running ads (anecdotal)

-
When to boost

Boost posts already performing well to gain more eyeballs

- Don’t boost underperformers “to see how they do”

-
Organic vs. ad creative differences

Organic top-performers can sometimes be adapted to ads with CTA

- Best ad creatives may not perform organically

- Ads should include clear CTA; organic need not

- Example: Two video ads for $4.95 class worked as ads but not organically

AI avatars and FTC/legal considerations in ads

- Risk: AI avatars posing as testimonials can trigger FTC violations

- Safer framing: spokesperson-style descriptions, facts, no false “I used it” claims

- Transparency: creator appears within/after AI segment to avoid bait-and-switch

- Influencer vs. AI: hired influencers still risky if they didn’t use product; AI definitely didn’t

IP/AI content scraping issues

- Case: AI-generated book tied to a documentary sold on Amazon without creators

- Options: Likely requires legal action; Amazon doesn’t pre-vet rights

- Journalistic exceptions exist but are nuanced
