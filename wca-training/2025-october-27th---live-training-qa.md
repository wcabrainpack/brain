**Topics Discussed:**

ManyChat Instagram Summit recap (30 hours distilled)

-
Overall takeaways

Social platforms are shifting from “social” to “media”

- Treat platforms like visual email lists

- Core strategies largely align with what’s already taught (90% overlap)

- Focus here on the extra 10% tactical edges

-
Email list emphasis

Grow your email list; send consistent offers (weekly)

- Rotate freebies when not running launches/challenges

- Example: turn book chapters into micro-freebies

Content strategy: growth vs. nurture

-
Definitions

Growth content: engineered to reach new people (hook-first)

- Nurture content: deeper concepts/education for existing audience

- Common mistake: producing only nurture content limits growth

-
Shareability and algorithm signals

First 3 seconds are critical

- Metrics prioritized: shares > comments/likes/follows

- Watch time in first seconds influences distribution

Hook tactics and creative formats

- Visual and written hooks

- Negative hooks outperform positive (10–40x reach possible)

-
Positive/negative juxtaposition on-screen

Side-by-side “best vs worst” or “before vs after” frames

- Examples across niches (food blogger, cycle phases, social approval)

- Apply to business: perfection vs ugly-first, pricing contrasts, etc.

-
Repurposing winning content with new hooks

Re-run same content; change the hook, entry angle, or setting

- Using props and pattern interrupts (markers, Rubik’s cube)

- “Cringy” content often performs; calibrate to brand fit

-
Avoid CUB issues in content

Confusing, Unbelievable, Boring

Shareable/educational content notes

- Educational can work if packaged with strong hook and simplicity

- Keep language simple; avoid heavy concepts in short-form

- Make long-form feel like FaceTime/Zoom with a friend

Formats and mix

- Combine Reels and Carousels (roughly 50/50 recommended by speakers)

- Re-record custom hooks for repurposed YouTube clips

- YouTube as separate long-form discovery engine

Podcast positioning

- Primarily nurture/mid-funnel

- Rarely grows audience directly

- Converts existing followers to deeper engagement

Affiliate revenue insights

- Many creators earn 50–80% from affiliate deals

- Case: fitness/menopause niche leveraging recurring affiliate offers

- Promote products already used at home; recurring commissions

Social proof and testimonials

- Use testimonials heavily in content and ads

ManyChat/DM automation tactics

- Single keyword usable across Stories, DMs, Posts

-
Capture email directly in IG DMs

Then send link to landing page (email entered again)

- Benefits: pixeling, double-qualification

-
Ads integration

Run via Ads Manager with CTA button + comment keyword

- New instruction: “Follow me and then comment” to ensure inbox delivery and gain followers

-
24-hour follow-up DMs

Boosts conversion; B2B: 1 follow-up; B2C: 2–3 follow-ups

-
Email cross-promotion

Send an IG post to your email list to drive engagement

Growth content Q&A highlights

- Growth content ≠ “going viral,” it’s hook-first to reach new people

- Ratio guidance not fixed; sprinkle growth hooks regularly

- Viral-for-you concept: optimize against your own baseline

- Repurposing YouTube to IG needs bespoke hooks

- Keep same offers across platforms; route all roads to email list

- Email list as hub; crosslink emails to IG/YouTube

AI usage guidance

-
Use AI to:

Translate viral hooks to your niche

- Generate background B-roll for text-over-video

- Support hooks and packaging, not fully replace you

- Mix AI visuals with human voice/face for authenticity

Practical hook examples (adaptations)

- Perfection vs ugly-first launch

- Low-price churn vs premium, ROI-focused offers

- Authors: ugly first draft vs endless polishing

- Time/priority reframes without shaming (conviction, not condemnation)

Live content and platform notes

- “Going live” de-emphasized by speakers (except specific TikTok shop contexts)

Repurposing and simplicity principles

- Keep IG content simple, snackable, emotionally engaging

- Long/deep dives belong to YouTube, webinars, podcasts

MailerLite automation walkthrough (technical)

-
Time delays and scheduling pitfalls

Use “wait 1 day” plus “specific time of day” to avoid same-day sends

- Option: set time to 00:00 to force next-day cadence

-
Segmenting:

Add contacts to “completed workflow” vs “in workflow” groups

- Conditions/branches based on opens/clicks

Brand building definition

- Brand = highlighting what makes you different

- Stand out vs blend in; then tailor viral hooks to your lane

Mindset, nervous system, and performance

- Creativity flows from a regulated nervous system

- SMS method recap emphasis (sustain)

- Triggers and real-life examples (family stress, emergencies)

- Excellence vs perfectionism; optimize over time

- Managing daily triggers without shutdown

- Environment and regulation practices (e.g., music, supplements)
