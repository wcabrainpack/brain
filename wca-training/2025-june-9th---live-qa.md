Topics Discussed:

### Program Momentum & Mindset

-
Tiffany’s offer gaining traction

Topic: performance-based acceptance/anxiety

- Fear of “starting something new” vs. staying focused

-
Shifting from “hope” to confident expectation

Audience wants to hear from you

- Detaching self-worth from external acceptance

### Pricing, Offers & Sales Progress

- No sales yet, but increasing interest

- Pricing doubts (“Did I price it too high?”)

- Importance of clearly stating that something is for sale

-
Using deadlines, bonuses and early-action incentives

“We open July 8” language vs. hard cut-off

- Example conversion rates (3-5 % of list)

### Direct-Response Sales Strategy

- Necessity of explicit calls-to-action every day

- “Hand-holding” (aka helicopter/concierge selling) before the sale

- Micromanaging prospects vs. empowering clients post-sale

- Ethical balance: persuasion vs. manipulation

- Leveraging conversations to refine offers (crowdsourcing)

### Funnels, Automation & Follow-Up

- Retargeting ads with 3-day deadlines

- Lead magnets with expiring bonuses

- Email sequences, DMs, comments as automated nudges

- Using transcripts + ChatGPT to draft proposals

- Video + PDF proposals sent after discovery calls

- Follow-up timing based on prospect readiness

### Client & Program Structure

-
Group coaching vs. 1-on-1 vs. hybrid VIP

Voxer chat for individual help inside a group container

- Setting onboarding expectations (no spoon-feeding)

- Offering higher-touch upgrades for those who need it

### Time & Focus Management

- Color-coded calendar and blocked “work vs. personal” windows

- Avoiding the “research/course rabbit hole” trap

- Scheduling social posts via software instead of live scrolling

- Identifying underlying beliefs that create overwhelm

- Reference to “Time Optimization” training inside the portal
