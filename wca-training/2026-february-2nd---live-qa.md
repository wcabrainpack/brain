1. AI Tools & Platform Integration

Lovable (App & Website Builder)

Google Gemini & ChatGPT

- Comparisons between Gemini, Claude, and ChatGPT for specific tasks.

Otter.ai

- Real-time transcription of coaching calls.

- Using the chat agent feature to extract data from current conversations.

Manus

- A simplified, more secure agent tool compared to raw Claude bots.

Slack

- Using Slack as a secure interface to communicate with AI agents (replacing Telegram).

2. AI Agents & Automation (Advanced)

Claude Bot (Open Claude)

- Capabilities:

- Analyzing competitor content.

- Creating daily briefs, blog posts, and video scripts.

- Handling administrative tasks (uploading to Zoom/Thinkific).

- Researching and summarizing transcripts.

Security & Risks:

- Dangers of giving agents access to banking/main email (prompt injection, hacking).

- Necessity of "Containerization" (running bots in isolated environments).

Hardware Setup:

- Using a Mac Mini (M1 or newer) as a dedicated server for agents.

- Using UTM (virtual machine software) and Tailscale for secure remote access.

- Setting up specific user permissions (treating the bot like a human employee).

Internal Agency Bots (Jeremiah’s Team)

- "Jill" (Research): Creates outlines and business plans.

- "Megan" (Therapy): Developing a bot for trauma work and IFS (Internal Family Systems).

- "Son of Anton" (Coding): Based on Silicon Valley TV show character.

3. Content Creation & Marketing Strategy

Copywriting

- Prompting AI to write sales letters using specific persuasive frameworks.

- Using AI to generate Facebook comments and replies.

Video & Avatars

- Sora & HeyGen: Creating "Digital Twins" or AI clones to produce video content without being on camera.

- Faceless Marketing: Strategies for coaches who are shy or prefer not to be on camera (using avatars, slides, or audio-only).

- YouTube Strategy: Using AI clones to scale content production rapidly.

Facebook Ads

- Religious/Healing Niches:

- Navigating compliance: You cannot target religious groups directly, but you can use religious language in ad copy (e.g., "Biblical healing").

- Using Meta AI to check ad copy compliance.

- Budgeting: Minimum recommended spend ($15–$30/day) to test creatives.

- Ad Suite Genius: A custom tool Jeremiah built to generate ad images based on copy.

4. Business Models, Pricing, & Offers

The $5 Offer Model

- Goal is not necessarily profit, but acquiring qualified leads for back-end offers.

- Economics of ad spend vs. back-end conversion.

Pricing Tiers (Monica’s Case Study)

- Structuring offers: Low ticket ($5) $\to$ Mid-tier ($297 Group Program) $\to$ High Ticket ($497+ One-on-One).

- Moving away from selling single one-on-one sessions unless attached to a VIP program.

Launch Strategies

- The "Tiny Challenge" Model: A smaller, paid version of large challenges focusing on one-to-one interaction (reference to Richard Dyne).

- Virtual Events: Hosting 3-5 day events to drive sales into a mastermind.

- Relaunching: The psychological difficulty of launching a second cohort vs. the first.
