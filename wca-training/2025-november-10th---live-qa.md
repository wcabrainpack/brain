**Topics Discussed:**

Current results and experiments

- Launch of Instagram Ads Playbook course; early conversions before ads

- $10 micro-course for mom’s offer: 25–30 buyers/day; $180 ad spend -> ~$300 revenue; membership upsells

- Preference for low-priced paid webinars/challenges ($4–$10) over free webinars due to lead quality and algorithm performance

Micro-offers and messaging strategy

- Structure: main offer -> $4–$10 micro-offer -> narrowly focused ad hook

- Importance of specificity: pick one high-demand pillar rather than full system

-
Examples of hooks/pillars

Draft high-ticket offer in 90 minutes

- Sell high-ticket with low-ticket lead-ins

- Create content with AI

- Google Doc high-ticket selling

- Trend angles: “sell high-ticket without X,” low-ticket to fill high-ticket

- Market research and modeling competitors; tweak successfully selling offers

Web and delivery setup

- Squarespace site build; confidence and speed improvements

- Delivery flow for paid webinar: website page with embedded Vimeo; simple email with access link

- Access model: 72-hour Deadline Funnel window; extension on request; action-taker filter

Ad creative and AI content

- Using AI avatars (Sora) for hooks and mini ads

- Formats: avatar intro, Q&A with avatar, mini reels, green-screen presenter wrap-up

- Performance observation: AI-enhanced content increasing reach 5–10x

- Tools and availability: Sora reuseable characters; UK availability concern

Offer pricing, scaling, and metrics

- Split-testing price points ($4.90, $10, $17)

- Goal: cost per sale below purchase price; scale daily spend if profitable

- Example math: 1,000 sales/day at $10; pay off cards daily; build buyer list for program conversions

- Email sequence: 3-day follow-up tied to access window; in-video pitch post-training

Upsells and program ladder

- Core program under $5k sold via email/evergreen; over $5k typically requires sales calls

- Structure: core self-serve offer with optional upsell to 1:1 coaching via call

Accountability and timelines

- Personal goals: complete Squarespace funnel, revise copy, content creation, ads before Christmas

- Weekly accountability via shared Google tracker; gamified progress

Funnel review: physical product (Glass Academy candy cane)

- Goal: acquire new customers with a product funnel; use Shopify for checkout, ClickFunnels/Squarespace for pages

- Traffic plan: Facebook/Instagram ads; TikTok videos + ManyChat keyword “candy cane” to drive to page

-
Visuals and page feedback

Move video higher; add brand/logo; brighten colors to brand green

- Ensure key image loads; fix ClickFunnels image scaling/permission issue

- Add lifestyle shots (holding, on tree), emojis, ASMR/sensory angles

- Consider snow CSS/JS effects for holiday vibe

-
Ad creative ideas

Process clips of glass stretching, studio footage

- AI composites: holding, hanging, gifting scenes

- Fast-cut, sound-rich, ASMR-style edits; seasonal hooks

-
Targeting guidance

Retarget IG/FB engagers; build lookalikes; leverage 45–75 demographic

- TikTok/TikTok Shop likely stronger for impulse gifts; FB/IG cold targeting harder but try with strong video

- Use product videos as ads; don’t rely on static images

- Link strategy note: on-platform posts with comment links vs native video; run video ads directly

Platform and ads ecosystem insights

- Meta (Facebook/Instagram) ads favored for targeting quality using AI (LLaMA-driven)

- YouTube ads: lower quality leads, hurts organic; Premium users skip ads

- X/Twitter ads: improving; Grok integration may enhance targeting; not yet widely used in niche

Live vs evergreen delivery

- Observation: majority watch replays; evergreen equals live for attendance

- Paid webinars replicate “stickiness” of VIPs and better cover ad costs

- Plan: ship a new paid webinar every two weeks to scale memberships

Finance and scaling strategy

- Using Stripe cash advance loans (example: $50k; ~10% cost) for growth, smoothing cash flow

- Option to pay self and invest; caution about debt discipline

- User considering US business credit to replace day job income for a year
