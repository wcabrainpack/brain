**Topics Discussed:**

- **Importance of placing crucial information "above the fold."**

- Highlight additional bonuses and separate them from the main offer.

- Rearrange the page layout to lead with the most compelling information.

- Embed a video of Jeremiah explaining the value of the VIP offer on the page.

- Jeremiah had 10 people attending live, but only two were VIPs.

- Suggestions to increase show-up rates: offering additional incentives like PDF handouts, emphasizing VIP benefits during live events, and tightening follow-up communication.

- Transition from broad, general language to more direct, outcome-focused copy.

- Emphasizing quick wins and addressing specific pain points like writer’s block.

- Our approach to building an evergreen system that runs automatically.

- Focus on creating multiple evergreen webinars on different topics.

- Use of live events to complement the evergreen funnel, particularly for leads who don’t convert right away.

- The importance of focusing on pain points and making the copy more direct and less wishy-washy.

- Discussion on how to leverage audience feedback to continuously refine and improve offers.

- Discussion on setting up automated, evergreen systems for these offers.

- Jeremiah encourages trying out both niches to see which performs better.

- Discussion about how to make copy specific and avoid abstract or “fluffy” language.
