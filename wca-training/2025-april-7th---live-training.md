**Topics Discussed:**

- Connecting emotionally with your audience through marketing

- Importance of visual storytelling in landing pages

- Start with a Google Doc to write copy before using AI

- Use ChatGPT prompts for writing and design

-
Begin with competitor research:

Analyze ads and landing pages on social media

- Save examples in a swipe file

- Keep an email inbox for competitor email campaigns

- Misconceptions about swipe file ethics

- Swipe files help emulate proven emotional messaging

-
New ChatGPT feature: AI-generated graphics

Logos, banners, stock images, Instagram ads

-
Process for generating graphics:

Paste sales copy or links into ChatGPT

- Ask it to brainstorm & create relevant graphics

- Modify text, layout, or visuals

-
Example graphics:

Instagram Ads Masterclass image

- Webinar graphics, cartoon versions of yourself

- Carousel posts, stock images of people working

- Create toy-box style graphics (Barbie/Ken doll meme)

- Replace faces in AI-generated images using tools (e.g., RenderNet)

- Keep graphics simple with minimal text

- Use AI to brainstorm ideas before generating visuals

- Specify dimensions for social media or landing pages

- Use graphics to enhance storytelling on landing pages

- Emotional connection is key to converting leads

-
Landing page structure:

Headline

- Video or graphic

- Story-driven copy

- Use AI to combine swipe file content into original sales letters

- Importance of saving competitor content

- Focus on profitable, emotionally resonant hooks

- Look at successful competitors—not just peers

- Specificity and consistency in messaging are key

- Use client coaching sessions to discover content ideas

- Dopamine and creativity are linked; do fun things to spark ideas

- Pleasure in investing > pain of spending money

- Align marketing hooks with audience pain points

- Repetition in messaging is effective (reference to Alex Hormozi)

- Marketing is about solving painful problems with clear outcomes

- Consistency builds trust and long-term success
