### Copywriting Principles Highlighted

- Lead each section with positive outcomes after brief empathy

- Avoid double negatives & over-emphasis on pain

- Use clear micro-results, skimmable headings, bold text

- Story-based narrative outperforms bullet lists for sales letters

- Replace words like “never” with softer “no longer”

### Kajabi & Template Tips

- Save every section while editing to prevent data loss

- Most templates built by designers, not marketers—re-order blocks manually

- Learning curve: tweaking font sizes, images, section padding
