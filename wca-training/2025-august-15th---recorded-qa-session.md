**Topics Discussed:**

Ads and platforms

-
Facebook vs. Instagram ads

Same backend systems and setup difficulty

- Facebook better for 40–50+ audiences

- Instagram often better for broader audiences

- Recommendation: run on both platforms

-
Boosting posts vs. Ads Manager

Never boost posts; use Ads Manager

- Better targeting, optimization, and built-in AI copywriting

-
Objectives and strategy

Don’t run “grow followers” campaigns

- Build audiences and email lists instead

- Top-of-funnel content to strangers; retarget viewers with offers

-
Creative types that work

B-roll video with captions (often best)

- Talking-head videos

- Image ads, including AI-generated/cartoon-style graphics

- Use multiple creative types concurrently

Ad creation workflow and AI usage

-
Use ChatGPT to:

Brainstorm ad graphic concepts from landing page/sales copy

- Generate ad copy options

- Mix creatives and test across offers

- Ads Manager AI features for copy improvement

Courses and learning resources

- Instagram Ads Made Easy (2024)

- Facebook and Instagram Ads for Beginners (2025 edition; most up to date)

-
CEO Mindset Mastery course

Lesson: Learning to love uncomfortable situations (highlighted as exceptional)

Landing page and sales copy feedback (Karen)

-
Page structure and readability

Avoid blog post format; use a dedicated page

- Add bold headlines, varied font sizes, and sectioning

- Insert images/photos and a top-of-page video

- Use buttons and clear sections; reduce distractions

-
Visual/content elements

Include representative photos (self and audience)

- Use heading styles (e.g., H1/H2) for chunking content

-
Storytelling and messaging

Expand “Family Reset” with a vivid story scenario

- Add “transformation timeline” higher on page

- Shorten/bullet “what’s included” for punchiness

-
Contact and social proof

Provide call scheduling and messaging options (prefer IG DMs over email)

- Move testimonials higher on the page

Funnel mechanics and engagement

-
Reduce friction for free content

Deliver multi-video series on a single unlocked page (no login)

- Thank-you page instructs to check email, then link to one-page access

-
Interpreting low consumption

Many registrants didn’t watch: offer might be misaligned or steps too arduous

- Emails may not resonate; test subject lines/hooks

-
Experimental mindset

Treat everything as tests; look for action-driving “triggers”

- Example contrast: low webinar show-up for broad “7-figure funnel” vs. high for specific “how to run Facebook ads”

Mindset and launching

-
“Not good enough to launch” fallacy

Successful people launch before feeling ready

- Embrace discomfort; take action despite imperfection

- Recommendation to watch CEO Mindset Mastery lessons 1–2

Audio/AI tools

-
11 Labs overview

Affordable text-to-speech and professional voice cloning

- Workflow: upload sample, type script, generate audio for Reels

Landing page feedback (Wade)

-
Headline language

“Emotional flooding” is therapy jargon; rewrite to plain, relatable language

- Example alternatives: From blow ups to breakthroughs; How to stay calm and connected; Talk without fighting

-
Messaging clarity

Explain pain without requiring intellectual hoops

- Generate multiple lay terms for “emotional flooding” (e.g., mentally maxed out, brain freeze)

-
Structure and flow

Move “Imagine” benefits section higher

- Add testimonials higher on page

- Keep Bible-based note to attract right audience

- Call out that common advice (e.g., “take a break”) often fails; present your alternative

- Overall layout praised for sections/design; primary tweak is audience language alignment
