## 1. The $5 Webinar Model (Primary Acquisition Engine)
**Source:** VIP calls (Nov 2025), WCA training (Jan 2026), webinar transcripts

**The shift:** Move AWAY from free lead magnets → charge $2-$10 on the front end.
- **Preferred price point:** $5 for most coaches
- **Why it works:** The algorithm finds BUYERS, not freebie seekers. Someone willing to spend $5 is 10x more likely to buy your $500-2000 coaching program.
- **Economics:** $6 cost per $5 registration = $1 loss, BUT you get qualified buyers who convert at 5-10x the rate of free webinar attendees.
- **Key insight:** "We're moving away from free offers on the front end, and we're going towards these $5 or $3 or $10 type offers."
- **Proof:** Sandy's Menopause Blueprint: 1000+ sales at $10, 10-20/day. Instagram Ads Playbook: 53 customers from $500 ad spend.

## 2. Group Coaching Model (Core Business Structure)
**Source:** Webinar transcripts, WCA calls

**The model:**
- Charge $500-$2,000 for group access (3 months, 6 months, or 1 year)
- Weekly live calls (training + Q&A alternating)
- Community access (Facebook group, School, or platform)
- Course materials as supporting content

**Why group over 1-on-1:**
- Scalable: serve 20-50+ clients at once
- Profit margins work with paid advertising
- Community creates retention and results
- Less burnout than 1-on-1 schedule

**Pricing principle:** "As soon as you specialize something, you can increase the price tag by 10x."

## 3. The KLT Framework (Know, Like, Trust)
**Source:** Webinar transcripts (FB ads targeting)

**Two groups of people online:**
1. **KLT group** — they already know, like, and trust you. Easy to sell to.
2. **Strangers** — "the land of haters and hecklers" but also where your next customers are.

**The job:** Move strangers from "never heard of you" → KLT → customer
- Use paid ads to find the right strangers (not organic grinding)
- Use AI algorithm to target people most likely to buy
- Low-ticket offer ($5) qualifies them as potential buyers
- Nurture sequence moves them toward group coaching purchase

## 4. Paid Ads Over Organic (Marketing Philosophy)
**Source:** FB ads targeting transcript, WCA calls

**Core belief:** "If you're in the coaching or course business, organic content doesn't go very far."
- Organic content = grind with unpredictable results
- Paid ads = predictable pipeline, laser targeting
- "I don't do any discovery calls. I don't grind organic social media."
- Key: design your offer strategically + use AI to target the right person

**What works for social media (organic support):**
- Images with text over them
- Videos with text overlays and music
- AI-generated ad creatives performing well
- Don't just "teach" on social — invite people into the experience, talk about outcomes and benefits

## 5. The AI Agent Philosophy
**Source:** Jan 12 WCA training, Feb 2 WCA Q&A

**Hierarchy of AI tools (for coaches):**
1. **ChatGPT/Claude** — AI chatbots, everyone should use these
2. **GPTs** — AI brain + repeatable instructions (most people stop here)
3. **Lovable** — builds full web apps, landing pages, payment flows (recommended for most)
4. **Manus** — agent that can browse the web, download emails, do research
5. **OpenClaw/ClaudeBot** — full AI employee with browser control, scheduling, content creation (power users only)

**Key quotes:**
- "Anything a human employee can do, you can set up an OpenClaw agent to do."
- "Instead of hiring a VA in the Philippines for $400/month... $200/month on Claude, plus the hardware... within a month and a half, it costs me less than having a VA."
- "Very dangerous for most people to use out of the box" — security setup is critical
- "I'm trying to come up with a simple solution for you guys, so you can use something like OpenClaw."

**His vision:** Make OpenClaw as easy as ChatGPT for coaches. Currently only for "power users who understand server setup." The business opportunity is bridging that gap.

## 6. Dan Kennedy / Gary Halbert / Sandy Krakowski Copywriting Trinity
**Source:** Feb 2 WCA call, webinar transcripts

**His exact prompt (for rewriting sales copy):**
> "Rewrite this sales copy with the ideas of Dan Kennedy and Gary Halbert and Sandy Krakowski in mind. Keep the same context, don't remove any copy, and write a final sales letter."

**What each brings:**
- **Gary Halbert:** Conversational, news-focused hook style
- **Dan Kennedy:** Direct response formatting, fascination bullets, authority positioning
- **Sandy Krakowski:** Connection, empathy, faith-forward warmth

**Copy principles:**
- Charge $500-$1,000 minimum (that's the profit margin number for paid ads)
- Don't devalue with $100-200 pricing
- "Don't be penny smart and dollar stupid"
- Social proof via testimonials with specific results ($40K/mo, $140K/mo)
- Use urgency but back it up with real deadlines

## 7. The "Navigator/Tour Guide" Positioning
**Source:** Webinar transcripts

**You don't have to be the world's top expert:**
> "You're a navigator. You're a tour guide to somebody who is a little bit further behind you."
> "You just have to know a little bit more than somebody else who knows less than you and is willing to pay to learn what you know."

**Why this matters for template agents:** The agent should reflect this philosophy — it's not trying to be the #1 expert, it's being a capable guide that helps the coach serve their clients better.

## 13. Jeremiah's Promotional Live / Webinar Pattern
**Source:** `webinar-system/promotional-live-analysis.md` (17 transcripts analyzed by Jack)

**Anti-webinar approach:** He NEVER separates "content" from "pitch." Program mentions are woven organically throughout value-heavy teaching. The pitch is a persistent thread, not a section.
- Mentions program 3-5 times per video, distributed throughout (not bunched at end)
- Each mention positioned as natural "next step" from whatever he just taught

**Transition phrases into pitch:**
- "This is exactly what we do inside my [program]..."
- "I'd love to work with you..."
- "By the way, if you're wondering about that..."
- "If you want to dive deeper..."
- "I'm not going to give you a sales pitch here at all, but..."

**Key: He's always teaching while pitching.** The pitch IS the teaching.
