# Group Coaching Model

**Framework #2 — Core Business Structure**

## The Model

**Pricing:** Charge $500-$2,000 for group access
**Duration:** 3 months, 6 months, or 1 year
**Format:** Weekly live calls (training + Q&A alternating)
**Community:** Facebook group, School, or platform
**Materials:** Course content as supporting resources

## Why Group Over 1-on-1

| 1-on-1 | Group Coaching |
|--------|----------------|
| Serve 1 client at a time | Serve 20-50+ clients at once |
| Lower profit margins | Works with paid advertising |
| High burnout risk | Community creates retention |
| Limited scalability | Less burnout, more leverage |

## Pricing Principle

> "As soon as you specialize something, you can increase the price tag by 10x."

## Key Components

1. **Weekly Calls:** Alternate between training and Q&A
2. **Community Access:** Private group for peer support
3. **Course Materials:** Supporting content, not the main value
4. **Live Interaction:** The real value is access to YOU
