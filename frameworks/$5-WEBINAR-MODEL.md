# The $5 Webinar Model

**Framework #1 — Primary Acquisition Engine**

## The Shift

Move AWAY from free lead magnets → charge $2-$10 on the front end.

## Key Details

**Preferred price point:** $5 for most coaches

**Why it works:** The algorithm finds BUYERS, not freebie seekers. Someone willing to spend $5 is 10x more likely to buy your $500-2000 coaching program.

**Economics:**
- $6 cost per $5 registration = $1 loss
- BUT you get qualified buyers who convert at 5-10x the rate of free webinar attendees

## Core Insight

> "We're moving away from free offers on the front end, and we're going towards these $5 or $3 or $10 type offers."

## Proof

- **Sandy's Menopause Blueprint:** 1000+ sales at $10, 10-20/day
- **Instagram Ads Playbook:** 53 customers from $500 ad spend

## How to Use This

When creating a front-end offer:
1. Price it at $5 (or $3-$10 range)
2. Make it a webinar, blueprint, or playbook
3. Use it to qualify buyers for your higher-ticket program
4. The algorithm will optimize for buyers, not freebie seekers
