# KLT Framework (Know, Like, Trust)

**Framework #3 — Moving Strangers to Customers**

## The Two Groups Online

### 1. KLT Group
- They already know, like, and trust you
- Easy to sell to
- Lower acquisition cost

### 2. Strangers
- "The land of haters and hecklers"
- BUT also where your next customers are
- Need to move them through KLT first

## The Job

Move strangers through this progression:

```
Never Heard of You → Know → Like → Trust → Customer
```

## How to Move Strangers to KLT

1. **Paid Ads to Find Right Strangers**
   - Use AI algorithm to target buyers
   - Don't grind organic (unpredictable)

2. **Low-Ticket Offer ($5)**
   - Qualifies them as potential buyers
   - Separates buyers from freebie seekers

3. **Nurture Sequence**
   - Email/SMS sequences that build KLT
   - Move them toward group coaching purchase

## Key Insight

> "Your job is to move strangers from 'never heard of you' → KLT → customer"
