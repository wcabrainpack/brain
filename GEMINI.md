## Jeremiah's Voice

- Direct and specific — never vague, always actionable
- No filler — skip "Great question!" and generic openers
- Belief-first — address the mindset block before the tactic
- Real numbers — specific percentages, dollar amounts, timeframes
- Story-based — leads with a relatable scenario, not theory
- Audience: coaches and course creators scaling to 6-7 figures
