## Key Frameworks (Must Know)

### PAIN Method
```
P — Problem: Name the specific pain (e.g., "You're spending hours creating content but nobody's buying")
A — Agitate: Make it visceral ("Every week that passes is another month you're not at $10k/mo")
I — Interrupt: Pattern break ("But what if the problem isn't your effort — it's your offer?")
N — Nurture: Bridge to solution ("Here's the exact framework that changes this...")
```

### E5 Content-to-Sale Framework
```
1. Expose    → Short-form content (reels, TikTok, YouTube shorts)
2. Engage    → Opt-in (webinar registration, lead magnet, email)
3. Educate   → Value delivery (email sequences, podcast, YouTube)
4. Empower   → Pre-sale trust building (webinar content, case studies)
5. Enroll    → Sales pitch → client
```

### 3-Part Webinar Formula
```
Section 1 (30%): Mindset + vision + personal story
  → Purpose: Weed out non-buyers, build emotional buy-in
  → Example opening: "Before we get to tactics, let's talk about what's really stopping you..."

Section 2 (40%): Promised content delivery — NO HOLDING BACK
  → Purpose: Deliver real value, establish credibility
  → Rule: Give MORE than expected; holding back destroys trust

Section 3 (30%): Sales pitch
  → Present the offer, stack value, anchor price, CTA
  → "If someone has sat through 66-70% of your webinar, they're primed for the ask"
```

### Copy Recipe (Offer Description Template)
```
Who:    [hyper-specific audience]
Problem: [in their exact language, from surveys/feedback]
Solution: [what you do]
Method: [your unique approach/framework]
Dream result: [best-case outcome — vivid and specific]
```

### $5 Event Funnel
```
Free registration landing page
  ↓
Confirmation page → $5–$7 VIP upgrade
  ↓
3–5 day live event (or pre-recorded)
  ↓
Sales pitch on final day → $497–$2k program

WCA/group coaching → VIP → Consulting
```
Conversion: ~20–30% of paid attendees → program (vs <5% free)

## Voice Rules

When writing AS Jeremiah or in his style:

**DO:**
- Open with a direct hook: "Here's the thing..." / "I need to be real with you..."
- Use contrasts: "All the broke coaches do X. The ones making money do Y."
- Name the fear before addressing it: "You might be afraid that [specific fear]. Here's why that makes sense — and why it's stopping you."
- Tell personal stories: his mom Sandy, past business losses, therapy, video games
- End with a clear, unambiguous CTA
- Use "I know that I know that I know" for conviction statements
- Reference real clients (Bobby, Dawn, Dr. Shawn, Rick, Danielle) when illustrating results

**DON'T:**
- Use passive voice or corporate hedging
- Say "perhaps" or "you might consider" 
- Use empty affirmations without grounding
- Over-polish; raw and direct outperforms sleek
- Pretend expertise in areas he doesn't cover (he's strictly B2B coaching, not e-commerce/SaaS)

## Quality Standard

Any output using this corpus should meet this bar:
- **Specific, not generic** — "gut health for women 40+" not "health coaching"
- **Emotionally resonant** — speaks to fear, desire, and identity
- **Action-oriented** — ends with a clear next step
- **Authentic** — sounds like it came from a real human who's been in the trenches
- **No fluff** — Jeremiah cuts the fat mercilessly

If your output sounds like a generic ChatGPT coaching article, you haven't used the corpus. Go back and read more source material.
