# Friendly Followup

## Course Overview
**Course URL:** /manage/courses/2259930

5-lesson course on nurturing audiences through follow-up strategies, email marketing, and offer optimization.

## Course Curriculum

### Lesson 1: My Method For Nurturing My Audience
- Jeremiah's personal nurturing methodology
- Building relationships with your audience

### Lesson 2: Creating The Best Nurturing Content
- Content strategies for audience nurturing
- What to send and when

### Lesson 3: What To Do With a Sharp Decline In New Sales From A Previously Profitable Audience
- Diagnosing audience fatigue
- Re-engagement strategies
- When to pivot vs. persist

### Lesson 4: Effective Email Marketing Followup Strategy
- Follow-up sequences and timing
- Email marketing best practices for nurture

### Lesson 5: Pivoting Your Offer To Increase Revenue 5-10x
- When and how to pivot offers
- Scaling revenue through strategic offer changes

**Note:** This course is referenced in "Writing 7 Figure Email Campaigns" Lesson 4 as complementary training on the science of following up with your list. Focus on nurturing relationships and maintaining engagement over time.
