# Sales Letter & Landing Page Copywriting Made Easy with A.I.

## Course Overview
**Course URL:** /manage/courses/2949265

5-lesson course on using AI to write and optimize sales letters and landing pages, from structure to launch.

## Course Curriculum

### Lesson 1: Structure Of A Sales Letter
- Sales letter fundamentals and anatomy
- Key components and flow
- Conversion-focused structure

### Lesson 2: Using A.I. To Start Your Sales Letter
- AI prompting for sales letters
- Generating first drafts with AI
- Starting from templates vs. from scratch

### Lesson 3: Translating The Copy To An Actual Landing Page
- Converting written copy to web format
- Landing page design considerations
- Technical implementation

### Lesson 4: Optimizing Your Sales Copy For Maximum Results
- Testing and iteration
- Conversion rate optimization
- Refining messaging based on performance

### Lesson 5: Ready To Launch Your Offer
- Pre-launch checklist
- Final optimization steps
- Go-to-market strategy

**Note:** This course focuses on long-form sales copy for landing pages and offers, complementing email marketing with the sales pages emails drive traffic to.

**Relationship to Email Marketing:** Emails (covered in "Writing 7 Figure Email Campaigns") drive traffic to landing pages (covered in this course). Both work together in the conversion funnel.
