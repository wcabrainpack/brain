## Lesson 2: Best Time To Send Emails

### Checklist:
- ✅ Don't overthink your email writing.
- ✅ Avoid putting too much pressure on making emails perfect.
- ✅ Avoid the mistake of not sending emails due to uncertainty or fear of imperfection.
- ✅ **Broadcast Emails:** One-time emails sent to your entire list.
- ✅ **Autoresponder Series:** Series of automated emails triggered by a specific action (e.g., signing up for a lead magnet).
- ✅ **Event Series:** Emails sent before and after a live event.
- ✅ Aim to send broadcasts at least once a day, sometimes twice a day.
- ✅ Pre-write series of emails for lead magnets (autoresponders).
- ✅ Send pre-event and post-event email sequences for live events.
- ✅ Segment your list based on actions (lead magnets, events, courses).
- ✅ Use Deadline Funnel for limited-time offers.
- ✅ Send 5 emails in 24 hours leading up to live events (day before, morning of, 1 hour, 15 min, "We're Live").
- ✅ Understand most people will ignore emails - consistency beats perfection.

### Summary:
Don't overthink email writing. The worst thing you can do is NOT send emails due to fear of imperfection. Consistent communication is more effective than perfect messages.

### Key Email Types & Timing:
**1. Broadcast Emails** - Daily (sometimes 2x/day) to entire list for content/offers
**2. Autoresponder Series** - 4-7 day sequence after lead magnet signup
**3. Event Series** - Pre-event (5 reminder emails) + Post-event (4-day deadline sequence)

### Autoresponder Sequence (4-Day Example with Deadline):
- Day 1: Thank you + access to lead magnet
- Day 2: 2 emails (reminder + offer intro)
- Day 3: 2 emails (sales pitches)
- Day 4: 3 emails (final hours before deadline)

### Live Event Email Sequence:
**Post-Signup (Daily until event):** 4 reminder emails
**Event Day (5 emails in 24h):**
- Day before
- Morning of
- 1 hour before
- 15 minutes before  
- "We're Live" (at start time)
- Immediately after: Recap with bullet points

**Post-Event (4-day deadline):**
- Day after: Recording + offer
- Next 2 days: 2 emails/day about offer
- Final day: 3 emails in last 3 hours

### Key Insights:
- Most people will ignore your emails - this is normal
- Sending FEWER emails does NOT improve engagement
- The people who open every email become your clients
- Stay consistent to build a 7-figure business
- Use segmentation to avoid redundant emails

## Lesson 3: Writing The Actual Emails

### Summary:
This lesson demonstrates hands-on techniques for using AI (Claude 3.5 Sonnet via Poe.com) to write different types of marketing emails. The core principle: each email should have ONE clear goal and call to action.

### Key Principles:
- **Single Purpose Rule:** Each email should accomplish ONE thing only - no multiple CTAs
- **AI as 80% Solution:** AI writes ~80% of the email, you add the final 20% polish
- **Consistency > Perfection:** Regular sending beats waiting for the "perfect" email
- **Value + Offers Balance:** Send more valuable content than sales pitches

### Email Types Covered:

**1. Webinar Follow-Up Emails**
- Thank you + registration confirmation
- Include: Time, Zoom link, bonus materials access
- Engagement instructions: Take notes, block time, find quiet place, camera on
- Same core info, different subject lines for reminders

**2. Live Event Reminder Sequence**
- Day before: "Training starts tomorrow at 2pm"
- 1 hour before: "Going live in one hour" + 60-second quick prep
- 15 minutes before: Final prep reminder
- At start time: "We are live now"
- After event (for no-shows): Replay available + what they missed

**3. Promotional Emails Using AI Brainstorming**
Process:
1. Paste sales letter/landing page into Claude
2. Ask: "Brainstorm 20 different email angles to promote this event"
3. Choose one angle (time, social proof, future self, market demand, etc.)
4. Ask: "Write persuasive email based on [chosen angle]"
5. Refine: "Make it more FOMO-inducing / use less statistical numbers"
6. Review: "Act as expert in email marketing, assess and improve this"

**Example Angles:**
- Time freedom angle
- Market demand angle
- Social proof angle  
- Hidden cost angle
- Behind-the-scenes angle
- Common mistake angle
- Expert shortcut angle

**4. Personal Story/Behind-the-Scenes Emails**
- Share relatable moments (e.g., "Building Lego towers with my 2-year-old while course sales came in")
- Emphasize time freedom and automated systems
- Make it about THEIR outcome, not just your success

**5. Content Promotion Emails (Podcast/YouTube/Instagram)**
Process:
1. Take transcript of content
2. "Generate 20 unique angles for email to promote this podcast episode"
3. Choose angle (e.g., "live vs evergreen vs hybrid")
4. "Write email based on this angle"
5. "Adjust to lead with benefits, not controversy"
6. "Make sure it's clear it's promoting a podcast episode"
7. Rewrite in your own words for final 20%

**Alternative Method:**
- "Generate 20 Instagram reel ideas based on this topic"
- Then: "Write an email to promote the above reels"
- Reverse engineer content ideas into email narratives

**6. Using Existing Emails as Templates**
- Find great emails from your swipe file
- "Rewrite the above using this template below, but for [new context/topic]"
- Paste your new topic/transcript
- AI adapts the proven structure to your content

### AI Prompting Techniques Demonstrated:

**Brainstorming:**
- "Brainstorm 20 different email angles for..."
- "Generate 20 unique angles we could take..."
- "Create 20 Instagram reel ideas based on..."

**Refinement:**
- "Make it more FOMO-inducing"
- "Use less statistical numbers and simpler language"
- "Don't use testimonies, rewrite without them"
- "Make it lead with benefits, not controversy"
- "Finish the email below but use what I wrote verbatim to start it"

**Expert Review:**
- "Act as an expert in email marketing and assess the email above, then provide improvement recommendations based on direct response copywriting"

**Repurposing:**
- "Take the transcript below and generate 20 unique angles for the email"
- "Rewrite the above based on the template below but for the context and topic of [new content]"

### Editing & Personalization:
- Always ask yourself: "Would I want to read this email?"
- Rewrite AI output in your own words
- AI should be a brainstorming and drafting tool, not final output
- Add your personal voice, tone, and style
- Test: "Is this how I would say it?"

### Tools Mentioned:
- **Claude 3.5 Sonnet** (primary email writing AI)
- **Poe.com** ($20/month) - preferred interface for Claude
- **Abacus.AI** (alternative, similar functionality)
- **Llama** (better for Instagram/social content)

### Workflow Summary:
1. Define the ONE goal of the email
2. Provide context to AI (sales letter, transcript, existing email)
3. Brainstorm 20 angles
4. Choose best angle and generate draft
5. Ask AI to assess and improve
6. Edit/refine with specific requests
7. Rewrite final version in your own words
8. Ship it - consistency beats perfection

## Lesson 4: Analyzing How Your Emails Are Performing

### Summary:
Learn to use email analytics to measure performance, optimize campaigns, and make data-driven decisions. Focus on engaged subscribers and consistent delivery rather than fear of unsubscribes.

### Key Metrics to Track:

**Open Rates:**
- **Good:** 35%+
- **Excellent:** 45-55%+
- Higher open rates indicate more qualified/engaged audience

**Click-Through Rates (CTR):**
- Number of people who clicked links in emails
- Tells you which content resonates

**Click-to-Open Rate:**
- Percentage of people who clicked after opening
- High click-to-open = strong email content/offer

### Where to Find Analytics:

**Campaign Analytics:**
- View all sent campaigns (broadcasts)
- Compare open rates and CTR across emails
- Identify high-performing subject lines and topics

**Autoresponder Analytics:**
- Review individual email performance in sequences
- Track each email's open rate and clicks
- Spot anomalies and trends

**Tools Mentioned:**
- MailerLite
- AWeber (Messages → Broadcasts)
- MailChimp
- **Mail Meteor Spam Checker** (mailmeteor.com) - Check emails for spam-triggering words

### How to Analyze Performance:

**1. Identify Trends:**
- Which topics get highest opens? (e.g., "evergreen strategy" vs "live launches")
- Which subject lines perform best?
- What content gets most clicks?

**2. Spot Anomalies:**
- Emails with unusually high/low performance
- Topics that consistently outperform others
- Time of day/day of week patterns

**3. Segment Analysis:**
- Compare performance across different audience segments
- Facebook ads campaigns vs organic list vs client list
- Higher open rates = more qualified audience

**Example Insights from Analytics:**
- "Picking a course topic" emails → high opens + high clicks
- "Evergreen strategy" → higher engagement than "live launches"  
- Emails about finding profitable niches → very high interest

### Optimization Strategies:

**Resend High-Performers:**
- Emails that worked well 6 months ago can be resent
- New subscribers haven't seen them
- Proven content = reliable results

**Test Subject Lines:**
- Compare similar emails with different subject lines
- Use data to refine future subject line strategies

**Repurpose Winning Content:**
- Identify topics with consistently high engagement
- Create more content on those themes
- Use AI to generate variations

**Spam Word Checker:**
- Use Mail Meteor spam checker to identify flagged words
- ~1,000 words can trigger spam filters
- Limit (don't eliminate) spam words - natural language matters
- Don't obsess, but be aware

### Conversion Tracking:

**Track Beyond Opens:**
- How many leads converted to sales?
- Which campaigns drive the most revenue?
- Evergreen campaign ROI over time

**Data-Driven Decisions:**
- Use conversion data to refine strategies
- Double down on what works
- Eliminate or improve what doesn't

### The Nurture Philosophy:

**Email Consistently:**
- Write your list multiple times per week
- Don't be "precious" with your list
- Use the asset you've built

**Focus on the Engaged:**
- Let uninterested people unsubscribe
- Focus on those who open every email
- Those are your future clients

**Balance Content Types:**
- Videos
- Written content
- Lead magnets
- Offers/sales pitches
- Mix of value + offers (not salesy all the time)

**Build Relationships:**
- Stay top of mind through frequency
- Deliver value consistently
- Trust = sales over time

### Mindset Shifts:

**Stop Being Afraid:**
- Don't fear unsubscribes
- Don't be overly cautious about frequency
- The right people WANT more emails from you

**Consistent > Perfect:**
- Send imperfect emails regularly
- Better than waiting for "perfect"
- Nurture through consistency

**Your List Wants to Hear from You:**
- They signed up for a reason
- They're waiting for your next email
- Serve the segment that's actively engaged

### Action Steps:

1. **Check your analytics** - Review last 10 campaigns
2. **Identify top performers** - What topics/subject lines worked?
3. **Spot trends** - What does your audience care about most?
4. **Create more of what works** - Use AI to generate variations
5. **Send consistently** - Multiple times per week minimum
6. **Track conversions** - Not just opens, but sales
7. **Refine based on data** - Let metrics guide your strategy

### Final Takeaway:
"Continuously show up top of mind to people as often as possible, and the right ones will stick and become clients."
